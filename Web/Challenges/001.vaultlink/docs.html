Partner Integration Guide

Resource proxy

Partner resources are fetched server-side through the gateway. Supply the resource URL; the host must be covered by your partner agreement (allow-list).

GET /api/proxy?url=https://rates.partner-forex.example.com/daily.json

Requests are tagged with a partner identity header before dispatch. The proxy also transparently relays Upgrade handshakes, so partner WebSocket feeds work through the same endpoint.

Real-time settlement feed

Partners on the v2 protocol receive compressed WebSocket pushes from the internal relay tier. The relay is not directly reachable from partner networks; connect through the proxy endpoint exactly as with plain HTTP resources.

ws://<gateway>/api/proxy?url=http://<relay-host>/ws

Session flow on the feed: identify, then subscribe. Administrative frames (AUTH) are rejected on partner sessions — they are reserved for the operations console.

Notes

  • Upgrade requests are forwarded to the upstream service as-is.
  • v1 partners pass their credentials in the URL authority.
